# Demo photo credits

The demo library (`docker compose --profile demo`) is made of photos published
on Unsplash and served by [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos). The
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) allows free use without
attribution; the photographers are credited here anyway.

Album names, file names and the dates written into each photo's EXIF are
invented for the demo. They say nothing about where or when a photo was taken.

This list mirrors `photos.json`, the manifest the seed job reads.
